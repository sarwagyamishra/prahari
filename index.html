Compliance & Statutory Safeguards

DPDPA 2023 MHCA 2017 §21 CAPF Welfare Regs

Non-Punitive Clause: Stress markers, self-check scores and biometric indicators captured by this system cannot be introduced as evidence in Court Martial, Summary Security Force Court proceedings, or as an adverse remark in Annual Confidential Reports (ACRs). Data collection is for welfare and force-readiness purposes only.

Right to Confidentiality (MHCA 2017, Section 21): Individual mental health information is disclosed only with consent, or where risk to life is imminent, per statute.
