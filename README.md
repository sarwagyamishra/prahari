# PRAHARI

### AI-Assisted Personnel Stress & Welfare Monitoring System

A prototype developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement 26186**.

PRAHARI is designed to help authorized welfare officers identify early signs of **stress, fatigue and workload-related welfare concerns** among personnel.

The system focuses on **early intervention and welfare support**, not disciplinary monitoring.

## Features

- **Personnel Dashboard** — wellness check-in, self-assessment and support resources
- **Welfare Officer Dashboard** — risk triage and personnel welfare indicators
- **Commander Dashboard** — unit-level fatigue and workload trends
- **Risk Attribution** — shows the factors contributing to a changing risk level
- **Scenario Simulation** — demonstrates different operational conditions
- **Wellness Tools** — breathing exercise and mental-wellness resources
- **Confidential Support** — direct access to support resources
- **Privacy-focused Design** — role-based access and separation of welfare information

## Demo Scenarios

The prototype includes three scenarios:

1. High-Altitude Border Outpost
2. Election / Riot Duty Surge
3. Post-Leave Recovery
